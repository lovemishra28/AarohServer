Read this before the numbers. The training hard label is a single crop sampled from each row's soft probability distribution — it is not the most likely crop. On this dataset argmax(soft) == hard for only 54.3% of rows. That is the ceiling on top-1 accuracy measured against the hard label: even a model that reproduced the soft distribution perfectly would "disagree" with the sampled label ~46% of the time. So top-1 ≈ 56% is expected, not broken. Judge the model by NDCG@3 and KL (vs the soft target) and by top-3/top-5 coverage.
